The Machine / Pepper robot / Research update 11 November 2026

Don't reinvent the robot.
Build what is missing.

We found Pepper in a cupboard after roughly two years without power. Our family mission is to restore him and develop a modern AI-powered hotel concierge, with college reception as the backup demonstration. We are studying existing work before writing any new robotics software.

RESEARCH PHASE

What is proven by others: Pepper has been connected to modern language models, Android realtime AI, ROS, cameras and extra GPU computing. What remains unknown: whether our robot powers up, which firmware it has, and whether we can reproduce those projects. This is not yet a working product.

Our proof-of-concept roadmap

  1. Restore: correct charger, safe battery inspection, startup, version discovery and authorised access. No blind reset.
  2. Control: five repeatable text-to-speech/head/gesture actions via supported SDK.
  3. Converse: five minutes of natural voice AI, with interruption testing and measured latency.
  4. See: test ten objects and presence detection, with explicit camera consent and error reporting.
  5. Concierge: use a fictional hotel knowledge base and answer at least 27/30 defined questions accurately; no invented hotel policies.
  6. Product foundations: secure device identity, central config, logging, graceful offline mode and a separate college FAQ profile.

Who does what?

Boris: designs personality modes, 30 guest questions and tablet interactions. Victor: tests, records demos, assesses reliability. Alexander: power, firmware, network, architecture and security. The AI tools help build and document, with human testing for physical safety.

What might become a business?

A subscription platform could provide approved customer-service knowledge, voice and vision AI, multiple personas, device monitoring and business integrations. Hotel reception comes first; college reception is the fallback. Commercial pricing, demand and reliable operation are hypotheses to validate — not proof of a market.
