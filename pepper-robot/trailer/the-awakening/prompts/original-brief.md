# Original brief — Pepper Revival: The Awakening

Transcribed verbatim from AT's briefing messages on 11 November 2026 (Europe/London). The main prompt was written for Grok Imagine as a 15–20 second teaser. Later direction notes from the same conversation are appended unchanged.

---

Here’s a cinematic prompt for Grok Imagine, designed as a 15–20-second teaser for our Pepper Revival project.

The story moves from abandonment and silence to human discovery, the first spark of AI intelligence and Pepper’s rebirth. The visual style should feel like a premium science-fiction film trailer, with emotional storytelling and photorealistic cinematography.

PEPPER REVIVAL — THE AWAKENING

Ultra-Realistic Cinematic AI Film Trailer

Create a breathtaking, photorealistic 20-second cinematic science-fiction short film in 16:9 widescreen, 4K quality, 24 fps.

Main character: The original SoftBank Pepper humanoid robot, accurately reproduced with its glossy white body, large expressive black eyes, friendly face, chest-mounted touchscreen, articulated arms and three-wheeled base. Maintain exact visual consistency throughout.

Visual direction: The emotional atmosphere of a premium Hollywood science-fiction film. Deep shadows, cinematic volumetric lighting, realistic materials, subtle film grain, beautiful reflections, dramatic camera movement and physically believable robotics. Start with desaturated cold tones and gradually transition to rich electric cyan, blue and warm amber.

SCENE 1 — FORGOTTEN (0–4 seconds)

A dark, abandoned storage cupboard in an old technology laboratory. Dust particles drift through a narrow shaft of moonlight.

Pepper stands motionless in the corner, covered in dust, head lowered, touchscreen completely black. Old cardboard boxes and forgotten equipment surround him.

The camera slowly pushes towards his lifeless face.

Atmosphere: lonely, silent and emotional.

Sound: distant room ambience, faint wind, a single melancholic piano note.

SCENE 2 — THE HUMAN TOUCH (4–8 seconds)

The cupboard door slowly opens. Warm golden light enters the darkness.

A young boy approaches Pepper with curiosity and gently places his hand on the robot’s shoulder.

Extreme cinematic close-up of the boy’s hand touching the cold white shell.

A faint electrical pulse travels beneath the robot’s surface.

For a moment, nothing happens.

Then a tiny blue light flickers in Pepper’s eyes.

Sound: a soft heartbeat-like electronic pulse, followed by a rising musical note.

SCENE 3 — THE AI SPARK (8–14 seconds)

Pepper’s internal systems begin awakening.

His chest touchscreen illuminates. Elegant streams of cyan digital light appear around him, symbolising a connection to a powerful modern AI intelligence.

Subtle holographic neural networks, data connections and luminous particles emerge around the robot.

The camera circles Pepper as his head slowly lifts.

His eyes regain their glow. His fingers move naturally. He looks directly at the boy.

The transformation is elegant and technically believable, not magical or explosive.

Sound: rising orchestral strings blended with sophisticated electronic synthesis. A gentle, futuristic system-startup sound.

SCENE 4 — REBIRTH (14–20 seconds)

Cut to a stunning modern technology laboratory illuminated by warm amber and cyan light.

Pepper is fully awake, standing beside the boy, with a confident but friendly presence.

He turns his head towards the child, raises one hand in a gentle greeting and speaks in a natural, warm robotic voice:

PEPPER: “Hello, Boris. I’ve been waiting for you.”

The boy smiles.

The camera slowly pulls back, revealing the two of them standing together against a subtle network of luminous AI connections.

Fade to black.

Final title appears in elegant, minimal cinematic typography:

PEPPER REVIVAL

Forgotten technology. Reborn intelligence.

Below, in smaller lettering:

THE AWAKENING — 2026

Essential production instructions

* Photorealistic live-action cinematography, not animation or cartoon rendering.
* Maintain authentic SoftBank Pepper robot geometry and proportions.
* No transformation into a different robot.
* Believable mechanical movements, realistic lighting and shadows.
* A genuine emotional connection between human and machine.
* No excessive holograms, random text, distorted hands or impossible mechanical movements.
* Natural pacing with smooth cinematic transitions.
* Original cinematic soundtrack with emotional piano, orchestral strings and restrained electronic elements.
* The final feeling should be wonder, hope and the beginning of an extraordinary adventure.

Visual references

The first film should make Boris want to bring Pepper back to life. The second should show him what they could build together.
