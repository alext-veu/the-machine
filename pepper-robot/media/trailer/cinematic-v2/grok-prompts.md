# Grok Imagine prompts — Pepper Revival: The Awakening

Seven copy-and-paste prompts for Grok Imagine Video 1.5, one per shot. Each is self-contained. See `README.md` for the full shot pack and other tools.

## Settings for every shot

- Mode: image-to-video. Upload the start image listed for the shot, then paste the prompt.
- Aspect ratio 16:9. Test at 720p, then re-run the best prompt at 1080p.
- Grok animates from the start image only, so each prompt describes how the shot should end.
- Generate each shot three or four times and keep the best take.
- Shots 2 and 2B can instead be made with **Extend** on the finished shot 1 clip, which keeps the cupboard identical. Extend stops at 30 seconds in total.

## Shot 1 — Forgotten · 5 s · image `frames/s1-forgotten-start.jpg`

```
A dusty white Pepper robot stands switched off in a dark, abandoned storage cupboard, head bowed, chest screen black. The robot stays completely still. Dust drifts slowly through a narrow shaft of cold moonlight and a cobweb strand sways. Camera: very slow dolly-in towards the robot's lowered face. Mood: lonely and silent, desaturated cold blue-grey.
Audio: quiet room tone, faint wind, one soft low piano note. No speech.
Photorealistic live-action sci-fi film, anamorphic lens, 24 fps, shallow depth of field, film grain. Keep the robot exactly as in the image: glossy white shell, round black eyes, chest tablet, wheeled base with no legs. No on-screen text or subtitles.
```

## Shot 2B — "Who are you?" · 7 s · image `frames/s2-door-light-end.jpg`

```
A storage cupboard lit by warm light from an open door on the right. A dusty white Pepper robot stands switched off, head bowed, and never moves. A boy of about ten with short dark brown hair, a black jumper, dark jeans and dark trainers walks slowly in from the right, seen from behind and slightly to the side. He steps over the cables on the floor, stops an arm's length from the robot and tilts his head, looking up at its face. He brushes a little dust from the robot's arm with his fingertips, then asks quietly, with curiosity: "Who are you?" The robot does not answer. Camera: handheld, following just behind the boy's shoulder at his eye level, settling into a two-shot of boy and robot.
Audio: soft footsteps on a dusty floor, the boy's quiet voice saying "Who are you?", then silence and faint wind. Only the boy speaks.
Photorealistic live-action sci-fi film, anamorphic lens, 24 fps, shallow depth of field, film grain. Keep the robot exactly as in the image: glossy white shell, round black eyes, chest tablet, wheeled base with no legs. No on-screen text or subtitles.
```

## Shot 3 — The human touch · 6 s · image `frames/s3-touch-start.jpg`

```
Extreme close-up. A boy's hand rests gently on a white Pepper robot's shoulder; his fingers press slightly and his thumb moves. The robot is still and its eyes are dark. For two seconds nothing happens. Then a faint thread of blue light runs under the glossy shell from beneath the hand, up the neck to the head. One eye flickers blue, goes dark, then both eyes come on and stay lit with a soft steady blue glow. The robot's head tilts up a few degrees. Camera: slow push-in drifting right, focus pulling from the hand to the eyes. Warm amber background.
Audio: a soft electronic heartbeat, two beats, then a rising tone as the eyes light. No speech.
Photorealistic live-action sci-fi film, anamorphic lens, 24 fps, shallow depth of field, film grain. Keep the robot exactly as in the image: glossy white shell, round black eyes, chest tablet. Smooth motor-driven robot motion. No on-screen text or subtitles.
```

## Shot 4 — The spark · 6 s · image `frames/s4-spark-start.jpg`

```
A boy's hand rests on a white Pepper robot's chest beside its dim touchscreen. The screen flickers once, twice, then ignites: a burst of white-cyan light erupts from the screen, a ring of energy expands outward, and hundreds of bright sparks and thin streams of blue light spiral out around the robot. The boy's hair and sleeve are blown back and his face is lit bright blue; he leans back slightly but keeps his hand in place. The robot's eyes blaze, its shoulders rise and its fingers spread open. The shot ends at the brightest moment of the burst. Camera: fast push-in during the flicker, then a quick pull back as the burst expands, with lens flare.
Audio: a rising electrical whine, a deep bass impact on the burst, crackling energy, strings surging. No speech.
Photorealistic live-action sci-fi film, anamorphic lens, 24 fps, film grain. Keep the robot exactly as in the image: glossy white shell, round black eyes, chest tablet. The robot does not change shape. No on-screen text or subtitles.
```

## Shot 5 — Revived · 6 s · image `frames/s5-awake-start.jpg`

```
Calm streams of cyan light and glowing particles drift around a white Pepper robot whose eyes and chest screen glow blue. The robot slowly lifts its head, blinks its glowing eyes once, flexes the fingers of both hands one after another, then turns its head and looks directly at the boy. The boy slowly lowers his hand and breathes out, amazed. The chest screen pulses gently. Camera: slow arc around the robot from the boy's shoulder towards the robot's front, about 45 degrees.
Audio: warm strings with soft synthesiser, a gentle ascending start-up chime, quiet servo hum as the head moves. No speech.
Photorealistic live-action sci-fi film, anamorphic lens, 24 fps, shallow depth of field, film grain. Keep the robot exactly as in the image: glossy white shell, round black eyes, chest tablet. Smooth motor-driven robot motion. No on-screen text or subtitles.
```

## Shot 6 — Rebirth · 8 s · image `frames/s6-rebirth-start.jpg`

```
A modern technology laboratory lit in warm amber and cyan. A white Pepper robot stands beside a boy, facing him, one hand raised. The robot turns its head towards the boy, waves its raised hand gently twice, and says in a warm, friendly, slightly synthetic voice with a British accent: "Hello, Boris. I've been waiting for you." The boy smiles widely and raises his own hand to wave back. The glowing network lights on the wall behind them pulse softly. Camera: slow pull-back and rise to a wide shot of the two together.
Audio: the robot's spoken line, clear and unhurried, over warm piano and strings resolving in a major key. Only the robot speaks.
Photorealistic live-action sci-fi film, anamorphic lens, 24 fps, film grain. Keep the robot exactly as in the image: glossy white shell, round black eyes, chest tablet, wheeled base with no legs. Smooth motor-driven robot motion. No on-screen text or subtitles.
```
