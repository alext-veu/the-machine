## Open-source integrations
- [ilabsweden/pepperchat](https://github.com/ilabsweden/pepperchat) — Pepper–ChatGPT conversational integration with a Python bridge; repository also links demonstrations.

## What Pepper was made for
A social robot released in 2014 to greet people, give information, perform short dialogues and gestures, run interactive tablet applications and assist in shops, events, hospitality, education and research. Early emotion recognition was limited inference from expressions and voices, not human-level emotional understanding.
